ASH ALI — SHORT BIO (KEYNOTES & PRESS)

Ash Ali is an award-winning British tech entrepreneur, investor, international keynote speaker, and co-author of the best-selling book "The Unfair Advantage" (Business Book of the Year). As the first Marketing Director of Just Eat, he helped scale the business from a scrappy London startup into a multi-billion-pound IPO. He now advises, keynotes, and trains leadership teams globally on turning big shifts like AI into practical commercial advantage, sales momentum, and human leadership excellence. Based between London and Southeast Asia; working globally.
