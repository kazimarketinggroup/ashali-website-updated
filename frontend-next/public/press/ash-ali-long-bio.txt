ASH ALI — LONG BIO (KEYNOTES & PRESS)

Ash Ali is an award-winning British tech entrepreneur, investor, author, and international keynote speaker with over 25 years of hands-on experience building, scaling, and advising hyper-growth companies.

He is best known as the first Marketing Director at Just Eat (now part of Just Eat Takeaway, a FTSE 100 enterprise), where he played a pivotal role from its early scrappy startup days through to its multi-billion pound valuation and IPO. He is also the co-author of the internationally acclaimed best-seller "The Unfair Advantage: How to Solve the Startup Equation", named Business Book of the Year at the 2021 Business Book Awards.

Over his career, Ash has founded and sold multiple digital companies, invested in and mentored dozens of early-stage startups across Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia, and delivered transformative keynotes for major brands, corporate boards, leadership retreats, and international conferences.

Ash's keynotes focus on practical competitive advantage, demystifying AI for leadership, sales and market expansion, and the human traits that drive breakthrough performance. He splits his time between London and Kuala Lumpur, working with ambitious leadership teams worldwide.
